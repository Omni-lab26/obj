## KQ1. Why can't students start or continue studying? (2022–2026 surveys on motivation, planning, "don't know how to study")

### Takeaway
Across the largest recent Japanese datasets, the dominant self-reported barrier is motivational, not informational: "やる気や集中が続かない" is the #1 learning problem for ~half of middle schoolers (東進 2024, n≈36,000), "勉強しようという気持ちがわかない" has passed 50% of 小4–高3 (東大社研×ベネッセ 2021), and "上手な勉強のしかたがわからない" reached ~70% in 2022. Students do make plans (~70% of high schoolers) but only ~23% achieve them, and the #1 planning worry is "I'm not confident I can keep up motivation after planning" (東進 2021, n=85,312). Out-of-school study time has been falling for a decade while smartphone time rises.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- The barrier students name is "sustaining" (継続), not "starting" per se: planning is common (70%) but follow-through is rare (23%), and the stated reason is motivational confidence. A study app that only helps make plans addresses the minority problem; the majority problem is keeping motivation after the plan exists.
- "上手な勉強のしかたがわからない" (~70%) and "何をやればいいかわからない" are closely related; the Benesse finding that 学習方法の理解 raises both 意欲 and 成績 suggests method guidance is a lever, not just a feature.
- Study-time decline and smartphone-time increase are documented in the same cohorts (Benesse, MEXT), so "smartphone displaces study" is supported descriptively, though causality isn't established in these surveys.

### Gaps
- Could not verify the 2024 値 of 「勉強しようという気持ちがわかない」 in 親子調査2024 (primary PDFs at u-tokyo.ac.jp / berd.benesse.jp are blocked by the sandbox proxy).
- Studyplusトレンド研究所's own motivation surveys (e.g. 「受験生の勉強に関する調査」) could not be opened; the site index (trend-lab.studyplus.jp) is blocked.
- No 2022–2026 survey found that asks university students directly "why can't you study" with percentages.
- ベネッセ「学習基本調査」itself: only the 2015 (5th) wave surfaced; no 2022–2026 wave found.

## KQ2. What share of students use study apps / timers / 勉強記録, for what, and which features do they want?

### Takeaway
Smartphone-based studying is near-universal among high schoolers (≈90%+ have "studied with a smartphone" in Studyplus-run surveys since 2018; 53.6% of high schoolers used 学習アプリ per 内閣府 2019年度), and by the 2025 受験 cycle 88.7% of 3,000 Studyplus users used AI in exam prep. However, **no 2022–2026 survey was found that ranks the specific features students want in a study app** (勉強時間の見える化 / 仲間 / 褒められる / ご褒美 / 計画 / スマホ制限); that remains a gap.

### Cited Findings

**Usage rates**
- 内閣府 青少年のインターネット利用環境実態調査 令和元年度 (2020年1〜2月実施): 高校生のネット利用目的のうち「学習アプリなどを使った勉強」**53.6%**（ゲーム 78.7%）. — [時事通信](https://sp.m.jiji.com/english/show/4385) **[secondary]**
- 内閣府 同調査 令和3年度: 高校生 勉強・学習・知育 **67.6分/平日** (above). — [resemom](https://resemom.jp/article/2022/03/01/66026.html)

### Inferences
- Because Studyplus is the de-facto 学習管理 app (10M cumulative members) and its own research arm runs most "what receivers want" surveys, feature-preference data is likely published on trend-lab.studyplus.jp / PR TIMES (company_id 47308) — these must be read directly; the sandbox could not.
- The 88.7% AI-use figure (and "メンタルケア" as a use case) indicates that by 2025 the expectations for a study tool include conversational support, not only logging.

- 内閣府 令和6年度 (2024年度) results could not be retrieved (cao.go.jp blocked).

## KQ3. Role of SNS: 勉強垢 (Instagram/X/TikTok), YouTube "study with me", 勉強配信

### Takeaway
The only Japanese prevalence figure found for 勉強垢 is old (2017: ~30% of 1,328 中高生 app users had/viewed a study-only account, used for 質問・記録・モチベーション). 2024 Studyplus data shows the structural precondition is ubiquitous: ~90% of 1,401 high schoolers hold a private (鍵) Instagram account and multi-account use is rising, but that survey did not isolate study accounts. Motive evidence for study-with-me comes from Korean/Chinese research (ambience, controllable peer pressure, emotional support), not Japanese surveys.

### Cited Findings
- 朝日学生新聞社×アルクテラス 調査 (2017-07, 学習アプリユーザーの中高生 **1,328人**): **3割**が勉強専用アカウント（勉強垢）を持ち・閲覧; ゆるい繋がりを「質問」「記録」「モチベーション」に活用. — [PR TIMES via resemom](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20170731/33851.html) **[old but only prevalence figure found]**
- 東洋経済: 「勉強垢」= 勉強専用アカウントを作り、書き込んだノートや単語帳といった勉強の成果をTwitterやInstagramに投稿する中高生（割合なし）; 使い方は志望校や偏差値などの目標を明らかにした上で学習時間を記録し、お互いに励まし合う. — [東洋経済オンライン](https://toyokeizai.net/articles/-/163054)
- アプリ解説 (appllio): 自分の勉強内容が誰かに見られることで習慣化できる、同じ目標の勉強垢の投稿を見て切磋琢磨する、説明文欄に学習内容や悩みを書き、コメント欄で励まし合う（解説記事、調査ではない）. — [appllio](https://appllio.com/instagram-study-account)
- Studyplusトレンド研究所 高校生Instagram利用調査 (2024-07-22〜26, Studyplus上で高校生 **1,401名**: 高1 279/高2 346/高3 776): Instagram「投稿する」**4割以上**、X・TikTok「投稿しない」約3割; 非公開（鍵）アカウント保有率 **X・TikTok 約7割, Instagram 約9割**; メイン・サブ・裏アカを使い分け; 2人に1人が年1回以上フォロワー整理; 4〜6個のアカウント保有者の割合が2022年比で上昇; Instagramのみでアカウント2個保有 **26.1%**. — [PR TIMES via Web担](https://webtan.impress.co.jp/r/prtimes/items/000000261.000047308); [resemom 2024-10-24](https://resemom.jp/article/2024/10/24/79283.html)
- Studyplus 2022 SNS調査: Instagramは **7割以上**、Twitterは **半数以上**が複数アカウント所有、投稿・交流用と閲覧用を使い分け. — [DIME](https://dime.jp/genre/1398474/) **[secondary]**
- 国立青少年教育振興機構 高校生国際比較調査 (2024): 日本の高校生で SNS複数アカウントの使い分けを「よく/ときどきしている」**5割超**、4か国中最高. — [Forbes Japan](https://forbesjapan.com/articles/detail/74809) **[secondary; verify at niye.go.jp]**
- Studyplus 受験トレンド白書2026: 受験期によく見たYouTubeランキング上位は教育系チャンネル. — [media-radar](https://media-radar.jp/mediapicks/news/studyplus-trend-research-2026) **[secondary]**

**Study-with-me / 勉強配信 (non-Japan research + Japanese first-person accounts)**
- KAIST study (30 SWM videos + 12 viewer interviews): viewers use SWM to create a personalized study ambience at low cost, to get **controllable peer pressure**, and to receive **emotional support**. — [KAIST repository](https://dspace.kaist.ac.kr/handle/10203/288565)
- HKBU comment-analysis study: engagement driven by motivation, practical study techniques, and a **sense of companionship**. — [HKBU scholars](https://scholars.hkbu.edu.hk/en/publications/assessing-study-with-me-videos-as-effective-learning-environments/)
- Survey of **509** Chinese undergraduates using Bilibili SWM ≥3 months: peer support → higher motivation, learning satisfaction, engagement. — [Frontiers in Psychology 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1582857/full); summary [Simply Psychology](https://www.simplypsychology.org/?p=87460)
- Survey of **384** SWM learners grouped by motivational orientation (goal/learner/activity). — [IEEE Access 2023 via DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/15b785d3106d48519ea19396a4f88eae)
- Japanese first-person: Classmethod engineer ran YouTube Live 勉強配信 200回・約611時間; main benefit = 「半強制的な習慣化」（配信を始めたら勉強するしかない）. — [Classmethod](https://dev.classmethod.jp/articles/study-with-me-200days/); [earlier post](https://dev.classmethod.jp/articles/study-with-me-youtube-live/)

### Inferences
- Japanese 勉強垢 behaviour sits on top of a documented multi-account / 鍵垢 norm (90% private Instagram, >50% multi-account); the "study account" is one more role-separated sub-account, which implies users want a study-only social space separate from their main identity.
- The reported motives (記録, 励まし合い, 見られているから続く, 切磋琢磨) are consistent with the SWM literature's "controllable peer pressure + companionship", suggesting the same mechanism transfers, but this is inference — no Japanese survey quantified it.

### Gaps
- No 2022–2026 Japanese survey quantifying the share of students with a 勉強垢, or of those who watch/stream 勉強配信, or their reasons. Search for J-STAGE/CiNii papers on 勉強垢 returned no hits (a Kobe University repository item [da.lib.kobe-u.ac.jp](https://da.lib.kobe-u.ac.jp/da/kernel/0100492587/?lang=0) appeared in results but its content could not be checked).
- Studyplusトレンド研究所 has run SNS surveys in 2022 and 2024; whether either asked about 勉強垢 specifically is unverified.

## KQ4. Smartphone distraction: what students say and what they do about it

### Takeaway
Small self-selected surveys consistently find that 90–97% of high schoolers have been derailed by their phone while studying (YouTube the top culprit), most feel guilt, and **more than half abandon any restriction tactic within 3 days** (塾選/DeltaX). Population-level data show smartphone time rising (高校生 138分/日; 小6 +22分 in one year) while study time falls, and 10代 ネット依存傾向 at a record 24.8% (総務省 2023). No survey was found giving the share who use スマホロック / 機内モード / アプリ制限.

### Cited Findings
- 塾選（株式会社DeltaX）高校生調査 (2025–2026; 回答者数・時期は snippet に無し; 同社の他調査は高校生104名規模): **9割超**が勉強中の「スマホ脱線」を経験; 最も脱線させるアプリは **YouTube**; 罪悪感を抱きつつ繰り返す; スマホ制限などの対策を試みても **半数以上が3日以内に挫折**. — [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000143.000116808.html); [resemom 2025-09-19 (related DeltaX coverage)](https://resemom.jp/article/2025/09/19/83373.html)
- ASCII.jp 自社アンケート (全国の高校生 **103名**, 任意回答): **97.1%** が「勉強目的でスマホを開いたのに別のことをしてしまった経験がある」. — [ASCII.jp](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/423/4423618/)
- PR TIMES release 2026-07-30 (主体・対象は snippet に無し): 「スマホを見すぎて、罪悪感や焦りを覚えたことはありますか」→ よくある **49.5%** + たまにある **36.9%** = **86.4%**. — [resemom](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20260730/200754.html)
- 塾選/DeltaX 別調査: 高校生の **8割**が「学校に行きたくない」と感じた経験あり、時間制限のない交友関係で「ソーシャル・バッテリー切れ」. — [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000128.000116808.html)
- 総務省 2023 調査（東北大関連記事が引用）: ネット依存傾向 **7.4%**, 10代は **24.8%**で過去最高; 2024年時点でネット/スマホ依存は疾病認定・診断基準なし. — [ナゾロジー](https://nazology.kusuguru.co.jp/archives/163297) **[secondary]**
- 東北大 川島隆太: 5〜18歳 **224人**を3年間MRI追跡、毎日スマホを使う子は脳発達が停滞; アプリ数が多いほど成績が低い傾向. — [PRESIDENT](https://president.jp/articles/-/63638) **[secondary]**
- ベネッセ親子調査: 高校生スマホ **138.3分/日**（2024, 2015比+42.5分）; スマホ時間が長いほど読書時間が短い傾向（小学生で明確、中高の追跡では確認できず）. — [Benesse newsletter PDF](https://www.benesse.jp/berd/special/childedu/pdf/newsLetter/newsLetter_20251020.pdf); [秋田魁](https://www.sakigake.jp/news/article/20251025CO0018/)
- 文科省 2025 調査: 小6 平日スマホ 1時間5分（+22分）; スマホ時間が長いほど正答率が低い. — [防府市 PDF](https://www.city.hofu.yamaguchi.jp/uploaded/attachment/142024.pdf); [琉球新報](https://ryukyushimpo.jp/editorial/entry-5439790.html) **[secondary]**
- カロリーメイト×Studyplusトレンド研究所 (2023-12, 受験生活調査 **2,399名**): 受験勉強に「没頭」するコツは「睡眠」「**誘惑断ち**」「食事」（具体%は snippet に無し）. — [PR TIMES via resemom](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20231225/108956.html)
- Studyplus 大学生調査 (2025-11-15〜20, Studyplusユーザーの大学生 **522名**): 受験生時代のクリスマスに勉強の「切り替え」をした **37.5%**, していない **62.5%**. — [AppBank](https://www.appbank.net/2025/12/22/iphone-application/2892539.php) **[secondary]**
- Tactics described (advice, not survey): 勉強部屋にスマホを置かない、入室前に収納、通知を切る、関係ないアプリを制限; タイマー式スマホロッキングケース（5分〜24時間）. — [rocket-boys.co.jp](https://rocket-boys.co.jp/?p=27973); [DIME](https://dime.jp/genre/1859585/); [ITmedia 2019](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/mobile/articles/1905/15/news111.html)
- 2019 Studyplus (2018 survey): 勉強中にスマホで注意された高校生 約3人に1人; 保護者の約8割が子どもを疑った経験. — [ITmedia](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/mobile/spv/1807/17/news101.html)

### Inferences
- The combination "97% derailed + >50% quit restriction within 3 days + 86% guilt" implies restriction features alone have poor retention; students may respond better to tools that reframe phone use (study logging, peer visibility) than to pure blockers.
- Because the phone is also the primary study device (90%+ スマ勉, 88.7% AI use), "put the phone away" conflicts with how students actually study — a design tension worth stating explicitly in the report.

### Gaps
- No figure found for the share of students who use スマホロック / 機内モード / スクリーンタイム / アプリ制限 (only "tried restriction, >50% gave up within 3 days").
- Sample sizes and periods for the DeltaX and 2026-07 releases could not be confirmed.
- MMD研究所 / モバイル社会研究所 2022–2026 releases on study-time phone use did not surface; only ownership data (モバイル社会研究所 2023-11: 中学生スマホ所有 79%, 小学高学年4割超 — [J-CAST](https://www.j-cast.com/2024/02/02477457.html?p=all)).

## KQ7. Study-app churn: how long do students keep using an app and why do they stop?

### Takeaway
No Japanese study-app-specific retention data was found. The closest proxies: >50% of high schoolers abandon smartphone-restriction tactics within 3 days (塾選), one-off rewards lose effect after ~8 weeks (Monoxer), and a general Japanese app survey finds 68.1% of people keep ≤20–30% of downloaded apps beyond a month, with "missing needed feature" the #1 reason and UI/UX the next six.

### Cited Findings
- 塾選/DeltaX: スマホ制限などの対策を試みても半数以上が **3日以内に挫折**. — [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000143.000116808.html)
- 株式会社Pentagon アプリ利用調査 (日本, 対象は学生限定でない): ダウンロードしたアプリのうち1か月以上使い続けるのは「2〜3割以下」と回答 **68.1%**; 離脱理由1位「必要な機能がなかった」, 2〜7位はすべてUI/UX設計起因. — [value-press](https://www.value-press.com/pressrelease/370762)
- Monoxer 畔柳: ご褒美の効果は平均8週間. — [PRESIDENT](https://president.jp/articles/-/61232)
- 東進 2021: 計画達成率 22.6% (plan abandonment as a churn analogue). — [Kyodo PR Wire](https://kyodonewsprwire.jp/release/202108118752)
- Non-Japan: Chinese university EFL learners (**n=632**) — dissatisfaction and frustration directly raise discontinuance intention of AI learning tools; frustration has the strongest direct effect. — [arXiv 2604.27506](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.27506); companion paper on intermittent discontinuance [arXiv 2604.27493](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.27493)
- Non-Japan: university lifestyle/wellbeing app study (**n=105**) — dropout reasons: no perceived effect, forgetting to use it, paywalls/excess ads, usability, lack of personalisation. — [Technium](https://techniumscience.com/index.php/technium/article/view/13021) **[attribution uncertain]**
- Studyplus as positive counter-evidence: 1,000万人 cumulative members (2025-07) and the product's own stated retention mechanisms — 記録のグラフ可視化、同じ目標のユーザーをフォローして教材・ペースを見る、達成報告 — described in company/press copy, not user surveys. — [resemom](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20250701/149227.html); [Lifehacker](https://www.lifehacker.jp/article/study-plus/); [HR Pro](https://www.hrpro.co.jp/miraii/?p=3462)

### Inferences
- Behavioural churn in this population is measured in days (restriction tactics) to weeks (rewards), so onboarding must deliver visible value within the first week; social accountability (勉強垢-style visibility) is the only mechanism with first-person evidence of multi-month persistence in Japan (Classmethod 200 streams).

### Gaps
- No Japanese figure for study-app D7/D30 retention or for why students quit 学習管理 apps; Studyplus has not published churn data in any source surfaced.
- Planned searches on 「学習アプリ 使わなくなった 理由」 could not be run (budget exhausted).
