## Key question 1 — 勉強垢 (study accounts) on X/Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, Threads: prevalence, content, hashtags, what users say it gives them, and downsides

### Takeaway
勉強垢 are a decade-old, mainstream behaviour among Japanese 中高生/受験生 (roughly 2–3 in 10 ran or followed one as early as 2017, and TikTok's #勉強垢 had hundreds of millions of views by 2020); the dominant content is hand-and-notebook footage, note photos, timers/clocks and stationery, and the stated payoff is 仲間感 (people like me are working too), being seen, and DMs of encouragement — while the recurring failure modes are comparison-induced self-reproach, 「映え」 displacing study, posting becoming the goal, and silent 垢消し after a failed exam.

### Cited Findings
**Prevalence and platforms**
- A July 2017 survey by 朝日学生新聞社 and アルクテラス of 1,328 中高生 found about 3割 (30%) had or viewed a 勉強専用アカウント — [resemom / PR TIMES, 2017-07-31](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20170731/33851.html)
- A March 2017 代々木ゼミナール survey reported that over half of 受験生 used SNS for study, about 5割 were interested in a 勉強専門アカウント and about 2割 actually used one — [resemom / PR TIMES, 2017-03-28](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20170328/29905.html)
- A January 2018 ライオン survey found 58.5% of respondents used SNS for study in some form — [resemom / PR TIMES, 2018-01-09](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20180109/39010.html)
- As of December 2016, the single Instagram hashtag 「勉強垢さんと繋がりたい」 had over 280,000 posts; the article attributes the spread of 勉強垢 to the study-log SNS Studyplus and describes 女子高生 posting answer sheets and handwritten notes — [J-CAST, 2016-12-03](https://www.j-cast.com/2016/12/03285093.html)
- A 2020 週刊アスキー piece on TikTok 勉強垢 reported 「#勉強垢」 at 3億3000万 (330M) plays, with typical videos being fixed-camera shots of hands and notebook, a visible clock to show elapsed time, and stationery/note-taking tips — [週刊アスキー, 2020](https://weekly.ascii.jp/elem/000/004/010/4010781/)
- A 2025 survey of that year's 東大合格者 (run by 西岡壱誠's team) found 32.4% gathered 受験情報 on SNS continuously and 41.2% non-continuously (≈73.6% total); 25.4% did not — [東洋経済オンライン](https://toyokeizai.net/articles/-/923269); reported also by [PRESIDENT Online](https://president.jp/articles/-/96052). Sample size not visible in the extract (verify).
- A DIME headline reports "高校3年生の8割以上が勉強するのにYouTubeを利用" (year not visible in extract; verify) — [DIME](https://dime.jp/genre/1903989/)
- 2018 TT総研 survey of 315 teens aged 15–19 singled out Studyplus as the place where "people who study gather", and reported a general teen preference to connect only with close people — [TT総研 / Trenders PDF, 2018-09](https://www.trenders.co.jp/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/trenders20180910.pdf)
- A cram-school chain announced an official TikTok (opened/relaunched January 2026) delivering short videos on 勉強計画・時間配分・学部別対策 — evidence that institutions now treat TikTok as a primary 受験生 channel — [resemom / PR TIMES, 2026-01-20](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20260120/168724.html)

**What is posted**
- 勉強垢 profiles typically state 学年 and 得意・苦手科目, and posts are study time and goals exchanged with mutual encouragement — [週刊アスキー](https://weekly.ascii.jp/elem/000/001/831/1831539/)
- Content categories named across sources: 勉強記録, 文房具, 勉強法, ノート写真, and タイムラプス of oneself studying — [appllio](https://appllio.com/instagram-study-account); [スタディサプリ進路, 2020-10-03](https://shingakunet.com/journal/exam/20201003000006/)
- 「タイムラプス勉強法」: film your desk with the phone, hands and clock in frame so the filling notebook shows progress; because touching the phone stops the recording, it "forcibly cuts off the smartphone temptation". The article counted 5.5万+ posts for the method and 880万+ TikTok plays — [スタディサプリ進路, 2020-10-03](https://shingakunet.com/journal/exam/20201003000006/)
- A later 週刊アスキー piece calls it 「ちょっと前に中高生の間で流行った『タイムラプス勉強』」, i.e. the peak has passed — [週刊アスキー](https://weekly.ascii.jp/elem/000/004/121/4121878/)
- A 高3 with 1.3万 Instagram followers posts 2–3 times a week and spends about 15 minutes per post; the motivation to keep going is DMs from followers saying 「モチベをもらっています」 ("I get motivation from you") — [高校生新聞](https://www.koukouseishinbun.jp/articles/-/10659)
- Qualification-exam adults use parallel tags such as #宅建受験生と繋がりたい and #行政書士試験 — [LEC](https://www.lec-jp.com/campaign/studyaccount/)
- Overseas equivalents are #Studygram and #StudyTok — [マネーポストWEB](https://www.moneypost.jp/1416724)

**What users say it gives them (observed quotes)**
- 高2 student: 「同じ気持ちの人が頑張ってるんだ」と思えるからやる気が出る (gloss: "knowing people who feel the same are working hard makes me want to work") — [スタディサプリ進路, 2017-03-27](https://shingakunet.com/journal/trend/20170327180365/)
- University student: 誰かに見られていることがよい意味でのプレッシャーになり、勉強を続けられている ("being watched is pressure in a good sense; it keeps me studying") — [マネーポストWEB via dメニュー](https://topics.smt.docomo.ne.jp/amp/article/moneypost/life/moneypost-1416724)
- Graduate student looking back: 真面目に頑張る人を勉強垢で見て負けないように私も頑張りました ("seeing earnest people on 勉強垢 I worked so as not to lose to them") — [uni-link advice column](https://uni-link.co.jp/advice/ELTIEi5acUgWZ0GG)
- A guide frames the payoff as being able to say things you normally can't (「〇時間勉強できた」「苦手な科目の成績が上がった」) and getting a feeling of 自己肯定感・達成感 from "これだけやった" — [find-model insta-lab](https://find-model.jp/insta-lab/?p=18647)
- LEC's promo explicitly invokes the ホーソン効果 (people perform better when observed) as the mechanism behind 勉強垢 — [LEC](https://www.lec-jp.com/campaign/studyaccount/)
- Studyplus app-store reviewer: 同じ目標の人とかも沢山いるし、身近な友達とも繋がれるからどのくらい勉強してるのかわかるし、すごくいいアプリだと思う! ("lots of people with the same goal, and I can connect with real friends and see how much they study") — [app-liv reviews](https://app-liv.jp/449926/reviews/?cur_page=4)

**Downsides (observed)**
- University student: others studying 毎日コツコツ made him think 「自分は何もやっていないな」 ("I'm not doing anything") — [マネーポストWEB](https://www.moneypost.jp/?p=1416724)
- Another user: SNSは“映え”を気にする文化なので、本当に勉強しているのか、勉強した気になって酔っているのか、その境目が曖昧になりがち ("because SNS is a culture of looking good, the line between really studying and being drunk on the feeling of having studied blurs"); posts prioritising looks over efficiency (付箋びっしり, シールでデコ) are common — [マネーポストWEB p.2](https://www.moneypost.jp/1416724/2/)
- After failing the entrance exam some users find it mentally too hard to continue and いきなり「垢消し」しちゃう子もいます ("some kids just delete the account") — [マネーポストWEB via dメニュー](https://topics.smt.docomo.ne.jp/amp/article/moneypost/life/moneypost-1416724)
- Yahoo!知恵袋 thread: 偏見なんですけど勉強垢作ってる人って良い大学に受かってるイメージがありません ("maybe prejudice, but people with 勉強垢 don't strike me as getting into good universities"); the search extract notes common criticism that it is 「勉強やった気分になるだけ」 — [Yahoo!知恵袋](https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q12239922997)
- Scam vector: a user reports DMs on their Instagram 勉強垢 from a self-styled 「受験アドバイザー」 pushing a 公式LINE add in exchange for a study-method text — [Yahoo!知恵袋](https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q13224123528)
- Anonymous attacks are named as a 受験生 worry: 匿名アカウントでの個人攻撃は特にこたえるでしょう — [現代ビジネス](https://gendai.media/articles/-/82869)
- General SNS疲れ mechanism (not 勉強垢-specific): a stream of others' showy posts inflates 劣等感や嫉妬心 — [マイナビニュース, 2026-01-26](https://news.mynavi.jp/article/20260126-3393057/)

### Inferences
- The behaviour is old and stable (2016 → 2026 cram-school TikToks), so a study app does not need to create the habit — it needs to host the artefacts students already make: timer/clock-visible records, notebook photos, and short hand-cam clips.
- The strongest stated benefits are *presence of similar others* and *being seen*, not competition. Feature implication: an "others like you are studying now" signal and lightweight reactions/DMs beat leaderboards.
- The main harms are comparison and performativity. Feature implication: make the comparison set small and self-similar (同じ志望校・同じ教材), default to private-by-default records with opt-in sharing, and avoid exposing raw totals of strangers in the home feed.
- 垢消し after failure implies a need for graceful "pause" or "archive" states and for separating identity from a single exam outcome.

### Gaps
- No post-2020 hashtag volume figures (e.g., current counts for #勉強垢, #勉強垢さんと繋がりたい, #studygram, #勉強記録) were obtainable; nothing at all was found on #今日の積み上げ as a study tag, and nothing on Threads 勉強垢.
- No 2022–2026 survey giving the *share* of students with a 勉強垢 surfaced (Studyplusトレンド研究所, TesTee, LINEリサーチ results on this specific point were not found; a 2025 Studyplus survey of 522 大学生 exists but its content on SNS was not visible).
- No first-person 「勉強垢をやめた理由」 essay content could be read (a note.com essay 「勉強垢とわたし」 exists at https://note.com/ko_to_ha___/n/n921de74ba377 but was not fetchable).
- No documented 炎上/晒し incidents specific to 勉強垢 were found; only generic SNS-risk guidance.

## Key question 2 — YouTube/TikTok 「study with me」「作業用」「一緒に勉強」 videos and live streams; 勉強配信 on Twitch/ツイキャス/17LIVE and live-study apps

### Takeaway
Japan was an early mass market for silent study videos (a 2018 report put a 1-hour はじめ社長 study clip at 3M+ views with a mostly 中高生 audience) and ツイキャス had a recognised 「お勉強中」 genre by 2018; today the genre persists as small, loyal live rooms (single-digit to dozens of concurrent viewers) where the value is companionship, a visible time record, and stranger encouragement — but no reliable 2024–2026 audience statistics for Japanese study streamers were obtainable.

### Cited Findings
- April 2018: Kotaku/Tubefilter reported 「勉強動画」 taking off on Japanese YouTube — creators quietly studying for up to an hour; はじめ社長 (≈6.1M subscribers then) had a silent one-hour study video with 3M+ views; viewers were mostly 中高生; US creators began copying the format — [Tubefilter, 2018-04-09](https://tubefilter.com/2018/04/09/study-with-me-youtube-genre-japan/)
- YouTube Japan's official blog (April 2020) promoted #StudyWithMe videos as something to study/work alongside, citing increased time at home — [YouTube Japan blog, 2020-04](https://blog.youtube/intl/ja-jp/news-and-events/2020_04_youtube)
- A Classmethod engineer who ran 100 and then 200 YouTube Live study streams reports 25 subscribers at the 100th stream, concurrent viewers reaching 6 on 2020-11-09, regulars accumulating, Japanese 受験生 showing up to study together, and that the stream "stays as a record so you can see how many hours you studied", with encouraging comments from strangers as a boost — [Classmethod 100回](https://dev.classmethod.jp/articles/study-with-me-100days/); [Classmethod 200回](https://dev.classmethod.jp/articles/study-with-me-200days/); [Classmethod how-to](https://dev.classmethod.jp/articles/study-with-me-youtube-live/)
- ツイキャス streamer 満点💊 (@kusuritte), self-described 「日本初！勉強ライブ配信し続ける男」, streams his studying daily, has about 4万 followers and has pledged to continue for ten years to August 2028 — [Twitcasting profile](https://twitcasting.tv/kusuritte)
- 2018 ASCII: ツイキャス has a recognised 「お勉強中」 category; streamers typically place the phone diagonally in front at a distance with notebook and textbooks spread on the desk (no face), and the platform is strongest with 10–20代; コラボ配信 lets others join — [ASCII.jp, 2018](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/001/638/1638576/)
- 17LIVE lists 5,000万+ global users and genres from chat to music, games and VTubers (no study-specific data) — [App Store: 17LIVE](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/17-live-%E3%82%A4%E3%83%81%E3%83%8A%E3%83%8A-%E3%83%A9%E3%82%A4%E3%83%96%E9%85%8D%E4%BF%A1-%E3%82%A2%E3%83%97%E3%83%AA/id988259048)
- "Lecture-style" 勉強系YouTuber dwarf co-study channels: 葉一 (とある男が授業をしてみた) had 2.01M subscribers as of Feb 2024 — [さんぽう進学ネット 2026 list](https://www.sanpou-s.net/column/10/); Japanese guides also describe a distinct 「一緒に勉強しよう」型 live-stream category without naming channels — [合格サプリ](https://goukaku-suppli.com/archives/53920)
- Goodnotes Japan maintains a list of "Study with Me チャンネルとライブ配信11選" (contents not retrievable here) — [Goodnotes JP blog](https://www.goodnotes.com/jp-blog/study-with-me-youtube-channels)
- VTuber study content (c. 2020): にじさんじ 郡道美玲 ran regular 高校日本史 lessons drawing on her teaching background; クレア先生 posts "勉強作業動画" studying alongside listeners; a MoguraVR roundup recommended calm long VTuber archives as background for telework/study — [MoguraVR VTuber lessons](https://www.moguravr.com/vtuber-lesson-movie/); [MoguraVR, 2020-03](https://www.moguravr.com/?p=124614)
- Benesse's StudyCast has run "人気のインフルエンサーと一緒に勉強に集中できる勉強ルーム" for limited periods — a productised form of study-with-a-creator — [App Store: StudyCast](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/studycast-%E3%82%B9%E3%82%BF%E3%82%AD%E3%83%A3%E3%82%B9-%E5%8B%89%E5%BC%B7%E3%82%BF%E3%82%A4%E3%83%9E%E3%83%BC%E3%81%A7%E9%9B%86%E4%B8%AD%E5%8A%9B%E3%81%8C%E7%B6%99%E7%B6%9A/id1474049638)

### Inferences
- The viewer's job-to-be-done is ambient company plus a time structure; the streamer's is a public record and small-scale accountability. An app can serve both without video: a "room" that shows who is in and their running timers, with chat muted by default.
- Because loyal audiences are tiny, the retention engine is *regulars recognising each other*, not reach. Feature implication: persistent rooms with the same cohort (same 志望校 / same time slot) rather than global discovery.
- The faceless desk-angle convention (hands, notebook, clock) is the privacy-preserving unit of "proof" students are already comfortable with; a camera-optional presence model fits Japanese norms better than face-cam.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 viewer counts, channel rankings or concurrent-viewer data for Japanese study-with-me YouTubers, Twitch 勉強配信, 17LIVE or ツイキャス were found; the 2018 figures are the only hard numbers.
- No evidence was found for a dedicated "勉強ライブ" app with meaningful scale beyond StudyCast's rooms; nothing on ポモドーロ配信 as a 2025 trend; VTuber items are 2020-era and may be stale.

## Key question 3 — Online 自習室 / study rooms (Zoom 自習室, Discord, StudyCast, 塾-run rooms): usage numbers and what students report

### Takeaway
Benesse's StudyCast is the only online 自習室 with public scale (累計130万 DL by Nov 2024; its own Feb 2022 survey shows online self-study use among 中高生 rising from 20.3% pre-COVID to 51.9%, with ~68% of 高校生 using it during school closures), while Zoom-based rooms from 塾 and volunteers remain numerous but small; students consistently describe the benefit as 「見られている」 pressure that stops phone-fiddling and the presence of 競争相手が画面の向こう.

### Cited Findings
**StudyCast (Benesse 進研ゼミ, free, open to non-members)**
- Launched for 中高生 as a free "勉強部屋" app — [PR TIMES launch release](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000770.000000120.html)
- Feb 2022 Benesse 「オンライン自習サービス利用実態調査」: share of 中高生 using online self-study services rose from 20.3% (Feb 2020) to 51.9% (Feb 2022), i.e. 2.5×; about 68% of 高校生 used one during 休校 (headline rounds to 約7割) — [PR TIMES, 2022-02-04](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000998.000000120.html); mirrored at [resemom](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20220204/78710.html). Company self-survey; treat as indicative.
- 累計 40万+ downloads and an App Store #1 ranking by May 2022, when it added import of study logs from other learning apps — [resemom / PR TIMES, 2022-05-16](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20220516/82415.html)
- 2022 Good Design Award as "自習室×学習記録アプリ" — [resemom / PR TIMES, 2022-10-07](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20221007/89065.html)
- 累計 130万 downloads since 2019 launch, per the Nov 2024 カロリーメイト collaboration which ran limited-time online 自習イベント — [resemom / PR TIMES, 2024-11-22](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20241122/128742.html)
- Feature set per store listing: rooms by 志望校/学年 ("志望校や学年が近い全国の仲間と"), stopwatch timer, automatic per-subject/per-textbook logs, 匿名の公開アカウント, スタンプ for encouragement, a 24-hour AI question feature covering 7 subjects, LINE or Apple sign-in, periodic influencer rooms; also cites a Google Cloud 生成AI Innovation Awards top prize — [App Store: StudyCast](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/studycast-%E3%82%B9%E3%82%BF%E3%82%AD%E3%83%A3%E3%82%B9-%E5%8B%89%E5%BC%B7%E3%82%BF%E3%82%A4%E3%83%9E%E3%83%BC%E3%81%A7%E9%9B%86%E4%B8%AD%E5%8A%9B%E3%81%8C%E7%B6%99%E7%B6%9A/id1474049638); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.benesse.studycast&hl=en_US)
- Jan 2025: added a 「ライブラリ」 feature recommending textbooks with sample reading — [PR TIMES, 2025-01-20](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001326.000000120.html)
- Developer case study (bravesoft) describes it as a "勉強タイマー付き自習室アプリ" — [bravesoft](https://bravesoft.co.jp/works/studycast/)

**Zoom / 塾 / volunteer rooms**
- スタディチェーン (HUSTAR) ran a free Zoom オンライン自習室 during the 2020 closures, 9:00–23:00, passing 1,000 cumulative users in roughly a month (April–May), with overseas participants; participants said having 競争相手が画面の向こうにいる gave them drive — [resemom / PR TIMES, 2020-05-13](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20200513/62414.html); how-to at [studychain.jp](https://studychain.jp/media/211/)

- A 中学受験 塾 note explains demand: "塾の自習室、いつも満席で使えない…" and home is distracting, so it pitches an online room — [雅学園 on note](https://note.com/miyabi_gakuen/n/n6ee9126b07d2)

**What students report**
- STUDY HACKER ("東大生もおすすめ『人に見られる』勉強法"): a university student who studies with friends over Zoom says 見られているという意識から、自然と姿勢も正しますし、勉強中に携帯をいじることも減ります ("aware of being watched, I straighten my posture and fiddle with my phone less"); the article also warns that observation speeds up practised tasks but makes unfamiliar tasks feel harder — [STUDY HACKER](https://studyhacker.net/workspace-for-better-concentration)
- Explainers from ポピー, 学研教室, 個別教室のトライ and WingArc list the same merits — no commute, mute-able audio, stimulus from seeing others — and demerits (unsuitable for unfamiliar work, camera anxiety) — [ポピー](https://www.popy.jp/topics/08.html); [学研教室](https://www.889100.com/column/column153.html); [個別教室のトライ](https://www.kobetsu.co.jp/manabi-vitamin/study/tips/online-study-room/); [WingArc](https://data.wingarc.com/online-study-rooms-27007)
- flat.social markets its Japanese "study with me live" on body doubling, with separate 集中ゾーン and 休憩ラウンジ — [flat.social](https://flat.social/ja/use-cases/study-with-me-live)

### Inferences
- The market has consolidated: one free, publisher-backed app (StudyCast) with 志望校-based rooms and anonymous accounts, surrounded by many micro Zoom rooms. Differentiation for a new app is unlikely to come from "a room exists" but from room *composition* (same goal, same slot), *presence without camera* (avatars/timers, as シグマゼミ does), and *rituals* (start/break/end).
- The "慣れない作業は見られると苦手に感じる" caveat suggests presence features should be togglable per session (e.g., hide status while doing hard new material).
- StudyCast's choices (匿名公開, スタンプ, no leaderboards in the listing) are a strong prior on what Japanese 中高生 accept.

### Gaps
- No current StudyCast MAU/DAU or room-occupancy numbers; no 東進/武田塾/スタディサプリ online-自習室 usage data was found at all.
- No Japanese Discord 勉強サーバー data (search results returned only English-language servers such as Study Together); no information on the 「集中」 app's 「みんなで集中」 feature or any "みんなの自習室"-type service.

## Key question 4 — Offline analogues that reveal needs: 図書館/カフェ/有料自習室, studying with friends, 塾 attendance tracking, school 勉強時間記録表

### Takeaway
Parents rank 図書館 and 塾の自習室 as where their 高校生 concentrate (70.7% and 54.3% in two small 2025 parent surveys), 塾 widely automate entry/exit notifications to parents (sometimes with photos and attendance-point rewards), and school weekly study-log sheets show the classic failure mode of accountability-by-submission: students 水増し hours when low totals get them scolded.

### Cited Findings
- 塾選ジャーナル survey of 100 parents of 高校生: place where their child concentrates best — 図書館 70.7% (1st), 学校の自習室 32.8% (3rd) — [塾選ジャーナル](https://bestjuku.com/shingaku/s-article/25415/)
- こども教材プラス survey (Oct 2025) of 140 parents whose 高校生 had used a 塾 自習室: most concentrating place — 塾の自習室 54.3%, 自宅 20.7% (sample is pre-selected toward 塾 users) — [resemom / PR TIMES, 2025-10-02](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20251002/158666.html); related release [2025-10-01](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20251001/158636.html)
- Municipalities are opening public rooms: 浜松市 opened city-facility 自習室 from April 2025; 川崎市 publishes 自習室開放 schedules — [浜松市 PDF](https://www.city.hamamatsu.shizuoka.jp/documents/166571/0801.pdf); [川崎市 PDF](https://www.city.kawasaki.jp/templates/prs/cmsfiles/contents/0000189/189532/zisyushitsukaihouvf.pdf)
- Paid rooms: a 札幌 有料自習室 for 中高生 with resident university-student tutors sells 1DAY, 1-month and 6-month passes — [ACU 札幌 2024-04 PDF](https://www.acu-h.jp/sapporo/wp-content/uploads/acumanabijisyuusitsu202404.pdf); a 柏市 monthly-membership room targets 社会人や受験生 wanting long focused sessions — [アガルート column](https://www.agaroot.jp/study-space/column/?p=437); a medical-track 高校生 testimonial for 自習室KAKOI — [jishusitu.jp](https://jishusitu.jp/medical-exam-highschool-focus)
- 塾 entry/exit systems send real-time notifications to parents by email/LINE/app; LINE-capable products include ビヨンド入退くん, QR入退管理 For School, LINE入退クラウド and Kazasu; some attach a photo of the student; some award 通塾 points and 表彰 based on attendance records; a deployment reported that dual-income parents especially welcomed the notifications — [shopowner-support](https://www.shopowner-support.net/attracting_customers/school/cramschool/entry-exit-control-system/); [一創 column](https://www.issoh.co.jp/column/details/16072/); [ITreview](https://www.itreview.jp/products/schpsaa/reviews/168317)
- A high school required weekly submission of study records checked by homeroom teachers; because 「学習時間が少ないと叱られる」 some students padded hours, and staff were not aligned on why they were checking; a teacher commented 学習記録を付けたから学習時間が増えるというものでもない ("keeping a log does not by itself raise study time") and urged looking at how students study, not just totals — [Benesse VIEW next](https://view-next.benesse.jp/view_section/bkn-hs/article06352)
- Another private high school redesigned its sheet to view time per week rather than per day, to push weekend use — [Benesse VIEW next](https://view-next.benesse.jp/view_section/bkn-hs/article06649)
- Benesse 教育総合研究所 uses weekly study-record data to feed teacher follow-up on low-accuracy items; it refers to a 学習記録表 (SP表) whose effect has "再現性が確認されている" (data not visible in extract; verify) — [Benesse BERD PDF](https://benesse.jp/berd/up_images/textarea/bigdata/Pla-36.pdf); [BERD 2018 PDF](https://benesse.jp/berd/up_images/textarea/2018%E5%B9%B4%E5%BA%A6%E3%81%AE%E5%8F%96%E3%82%8A%E7%B5%84%E3%81%BF%E5%9B%B31.pdf)
- 東進's own survey of top-performing students found their study habits were 「勉強を楽しむ」「計画を立てる」「優先づけをする」 (not social features) — [共同通信PRワイヤー, 2020-09-03](https://kyodonewsprwire.jp/release/202009033887)

### Inferences
- Offline, the valued ingredients are a quiet place where others are also working, plus adult-visible attendance. An app's "presence" feature is the digital analogue of walking into a full 自習室; "入退室通知" is the analogue of session start/stop sharing with a parent or 塾.
- The 水増し lesson: any metric that is *submitted to an authority and judged* will be gamed; metrics shared *laterally with peers* (勉強垢 style) are self-reported but socially calibrated. Feature implication: keep parent/teacher views summary-level and non-punitive, and make the student the owner of what is shared.

### Gaps
- No nationally representative student-reported data on where 高校生 study (only small parent surveys); no data on 有料自習室 market growth in Japan (the only growth story found was for China); no study on 「一緒に勉強する友達」 effects in Japanese samples.

## Key question 5 — Peer dynamics: public rankings vs 匿名/ゆるい繋がり; 競争 vs 共感

### Takeaway
The evidence tilts toward empathy and loose, anonymous, self-similar ties: 勉強垢 norms are mutual encouragement with 学年/科目 disclosed but names hidden, StudyCast built anonymity and stamps rather than leaderboards, Studyplus keeps sharing optional and frames others as 同じ志望校のライバル you follow, and teens tell researchers they want to connect only with close people — while explicit ranking is praised mainly in drill apps (単語帳) and the one stated harm of comparison is self-reproach, not loss of rank.

### Cited Findings
- Studyplus: users follow people with the same goals and see each other's logs, give いいね and comments; a weekly target shows achievement rate and followers' targets/achievement; "情報を共有するか否かは任意。自分のペースで勉強するのが基本" — [ライフハッカー, 2020-09](https://www.lifehacker.jp/2020/09/study-plus.html); features recap in [IT Leaders](https://it.impress.co.jp/articles/-/17660)
- Studyplus describes itself as used by 「大学受験生の2人に1人以上」 and a trade article cites a 62.3% usage rate among 大学受験生 in FY2023 (company-reported; verify) — [Studyplus 2025-09 release](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20250929/158178.html); [IT Leaders](https://it.impress.co.jp/articles/-/17660)
- Studyplus positioning: 日々の勉強を記録・可視化し、ユーザー同士でシェアして励まし合うことができる — [resemom / PR TIMES, 2022-06-23](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20220623/84068.html)
- StudyCast's social design is anonymous public accounts plus スタンプ encouragement in 志望校 rooms (no ranking is mentioned in the listing) — [App Store: StudyCast](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/studycast-%E3%82%B9%E3%82%BF%E3%82%AD%E3%83%A3%E3%82%B9-%E5%8B%89%E5%BC%B7%E3%82%BF%E3%82%A4%E3%83%9E%E3%83%BC%E3%81%A7%E9%9B%86%E4%B8%AD%E5%8A%9B%E3%81%8C%E7%B6%99%E7%B6%9A/id1474049638)
- 2018 TT総研 teen survey: preference for connecting only with people close to them — [Trenders PDF, 2018-09](https://www.trenders.co.jp/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/trenders20180910.pdf)
- 勉強垢 norm: disclose 学年 and 得意・苦手科目, exchange time/goals with encouragement — [週刊アスキー](https://weekly.ascii.jp/elem/000/001/831/1831539/)
- Students' positive framing is about sameness and company (「同じ気持ちの人が頑張ってるんだ」; 「負けないように」) rather than rank — [スタディサプリ進路, 2017-03-27](https://shingakunet.com/journal/trend/20170327180365/); [uni-link](https://uni-link.co.jp/advice/ELTIEi5acUgWZ0GG)
- Ranking is praised in a vocabulary drill app: ターゲットの友's ranking "lets you see your position and set rivals as targets" — [English Hub](https://englishhub.jp/app/targetnotomo.html)
- Comparison harm quote: 「自分は何もやっていないな」 — [マネーポストWEB](https://www.moneypost.jp/?p=1416724)
- Observation effect is task-dependent: easier when watched for practised tasks, harder for unfamiliar ones — [STUDY HACKER](https://studyhacker.net/workspace-for-better-concentration)
- 塾 attendance systems do use 表彰/points for coming often — a ranking-like mechanic applied to attendance, not to study totals — [shopowner-support](https://www.shopowner-support.net/attracting_customers/school/cramschool/entry-exit-control-system/)

### Inferences
- Design for 「ゆるい繋がり」: anonymous handles, 学年/志望校/教材 as the matching keys, reactions (いいね/スタンプ) as the main interaction, and optional sharing. Global leaderboards of study hours are not something any Japanese source praised; the one accepted ranking is in short-cycle drills where rank reflects skill, not hours.
- If a ranking is offered, scope it to attendance/streak-style behaviours or to small opt-in friend groups, mirroring 塾 attendance points.
- Reciprocity ("お互い頑張ろう") is cheap to support: auto-prompt a reaction when a mutual starts a session.

### Gaps
- No survey directly asking Japanese students "ranking vs likes" was found; no App Store review mining on ranking stress could be done (store pages blocked). Studyplus's actual ranking features (if any) could not be verified.

## Key question 6 — Sharing and "proof": what formats students share and which app share cards circulate

### Takeaway
The circulating proof formats are (a) a visible clock/timer in a desk-angle photo or timelapse, (b) a filled notebook page or answer sheet, and (c) study-log screenshots/graphs from Studyplus — the app J-CAST credits with starting the 勉強垢 wave; apps now compete on making the log itself the shareable artefact (Studyplus graphs, StudyCast auto-logs by textbook).

### Cited Findings
- 女子高生 post 答案用紙 and 自筆ノート; 勉強垢 took off around Studyplus — [J-CAST, 2016-12-03](https://www.j-cast.com/2016/12/03285093.html)
- TikTok 勉強垢: fixed camera on hands and notebook with the clock in frame to show elapsed time — [週刊アスキー, 2020](https://weekly.ascii.jp/elem/000/004/010/4010781/)
- タイムラプス勉強法: hands plus clock; the filling notebook shows progress; recording stops if you touch the phone — [スタディサプリ進路, 2020-10-03](https://shingakunet.com/journal/exam/20201003000006/)
- Studyplus logs by textbook (barcode scan) with time per subject and graphs; records are shareable in-app, sharing optional — [app-liv](https://app-liv.jp/505410049/); [ライフハッカー](https://www.lifehacker.jp/article/study-plus/); early description as "勉強の記録をつけてグラフとして可視化" — [@Press, 2013](https://www.atpress.ne.jp/news/42412)
- StudyCast auto-records per subject/textbook and since May 2022 can aggregate logs from external learning apps — [resemom / PR TIMES, 2022-05-16](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20220516/82415.html)
- Live streams double as a record: 「ライブ配信を始めることで、それが記録として残り、何時間くらい勉強したのかが分かるようになります」 — [Classmethod](https://dev.classmethod.jp/articles/study-with-me-youtube-live/)
- Influencer-level 勉強垢 posts take ~15 minutes each to produce (photo plus caption with ノート術) — [高校生新聞](https://www.koukouseishinbun.jp/articles/-/10659)
- A 2017 東洋経済 feature covered the 勉強垢 phenomenon (content not retrievable; verify) — [東洋経済オンライン, 2017](https://toyokeizai.net/articles/-/163054)

### Inferences
- The minimum viable share card is: elapsed time, date, subject/textbook, and optionally a photo of the page — essentially what students compose by hand today. Auto-generating it (with the clock baked in) removes the 15-minute production cost that currently favours 映え over substance.
- Weekly summaries and heatmaps were not observed circulating in Japanese sources; the observed unit is the *day* ("今日は〇時間") and the *session* (timelapse). Any weekly card is an app-side invention and should be tested rather than assumed.
- Because screenshots leave the app, cards need safe defaults (no real name/school) consistent with the faceless convention.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence of which specific app share cards circulate most on X/Instagram in 2024–2026 (hashtag/image analysis impossible here); no data on heatmap-style shares.

## Key question 7 — Accountability: 宣言, 罰ゲーム/ご褒美 with friends, 親に見せる, 先生に提出 — what works?

### Takeaway
Japanese academic evidence says public declaration works only through the relationship — frequency of contact with, and desire to look good to, the person told — not through the act of declaring; the institutional version (weekly sheets to teachers, attendance pings to parents) is widespread but invites padding when it is judged, and no evidence at all was found for 罰ゲーム-style peer penalties.

### Cited Findings
- 南山大学 study on 宣言効果: the effect is not produced by telling someone per se but is moderated by 接触頻度 with the declaree and 自己呈示欲求 toward them; a general reduction in procrastination from declaring was not confirmed — [南山大学 PDF](https://depts.nanzan-u.ac.jp/ugrad/JINBUN/shinri/manabi/item/ac41a799622e3efe6b28fdaad0bffa5b0b3b1b9f.pdf)
- Popular counter-view (Derek Sivers / Lewin lineage): announcing goals yields premature satisfaction; keep them secret — [ライフハッカー, 2015-10-25](https://www.lifehacker.jp/article/151025_be_successful/) (secondary, non-peer-reviewed)
- 広島大学: individual goal-setting for elementary pupils showed no specific effect on academic self-efficacy or outcomes — [広島大学 紀要](https://ir.lib.hiroshima-u.ac.jp/00015605?locale=en)
- Weekly log submission to teachers led to padded hours when low totals drew scolding — [Benesse VIEW next](https://view-next.benesse.jp/view_section/bkn-hs/article06352)
- 塾 attendance notifications to parents (LINE/app, photo-attached, attendance points) are standard and welcomed by parents — [shopowner-support](https://www.shopowner-support.net/attracting_customers/school/cramschool/entry-exit-control-system/); [ITreview](https://www.itreview.jp/products/schpsaa/reviews/168317)
- Peer-side accountability in practice is DMs and comments (「モチベをもらっています」) and "not losing" to visible peers — [高校生新聞](https://www.koukouseishinbun.jp/articles/-/10659); [uni-link](https://uni-link.co.jp/advice/ELTIEi5acUgWZ0GG)
- Streamer self-commitment at the extreme: a ten-year daily study-stream pledge — [Twitcasting: 満点💊](https://twitcasting.tv/kusuritte)
- A 東進 campus blog recommends stacking small goals for motivation (experience-based, not evidence) — [東進ハイスクール志木校, 2021-11-10](https://www.toshin.com/shiki/post/2021/11/10/%e7%9b%ae%e6%a8%99%e3%82%92%e7%ab%8b%e3%81%a6%e3%81%a6%e5%8b%89%e5%bc%b7%e3%81%97%e3%81%a6%e3%82%8b%ef%bc%9f%ef%bc%9f/)

### Inferences
- "Declare to the app" is weak; "declare to one or two people you interact with often and want to impress" is the mechanism. Feature implication: accountability partner = a mutual with high contact frequency (daily check-ins), not a broadcast.
- Parent/teacher views should mirror 入退室通知: binary, timely, non-judgmental ("started a session", "finished 90 min"), avoiding the weekly-total-to-be-judged pattern that produced padding.
- No basis exists in the sources for gamified penalties; treat 罰ゲーム as untested and potentially harmful given the documented 垢消し-after-failure pattern.

### Gaps
- No Japanese data on 罰ゲーム/ご褒美 pacts among students, on parents viewing logs, or on the effect of app-based 宣言 features; the Studyplus 2025 「切り替え」 survey of 522 大学生 may contain relevant items but was not readable.

## Synthesis — what the observed behaviour implies for a study app's social features (friends' feed, live presence, group rooms, share cards, rankings, accountability partners)

### Takeaway
Japanese students already run the social loop by hand — record (timer/notebook), show (faceless photo/timelapse/screenshot), be seen (small audience of similar others), receive 共感 (いいね/DM/スタンプ) — and the documented breakdowns are comparison, performativity and punitive reporting; an app should automate the loop with anonymity, self-similar small cohorts, presence without camera, auto-generated proof cards, lateral (not top-down) accountability, and no global hour leaderboards.

### Cited Findings
- Cross-references only (all items above): mutual-encouragement norm and anonymity — [週刊アスキー](https://weekly.ascii.jp/elem/000/001/831/1831539/), [StudyCast listing](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/studycast-%E3%82%B9%E3%82%BF%E3%82%AD%E3%83%A3%E3%82%B9-%E5%8B%89%E5%BC%B7%E3%82%BF%E3%82%A4%E3%83%9E%E3%83%BC%E3%81%A7%E9%9B%86%E4%B8%AD%E5%8A%9B%E3%81%8C%E7%B6%99%E7%B6%9A/id1474049638); being-watched benefit and its task-dependence — [STUDY HACKER](https://studyhacker.net/workspace-for-better-concentration), [マネーポストWEB via dメニュー](https://topics.smt.docomo.ne.jp/amp/article/moneypost/life/moneypost-1416724); comparison and 映え harms — [マネーポストWEB p.2](https://www.moneypost.jp/1416724/2/); padding under judged reporting — [Benesse VIEW next](https://view-next.benesse.jp/view_section/bkn-hs/article06352); declaration works via relationship — [南山大学 PDF](https://depts.nanzan-u.ac.jp/ugrad/JINBUN/shinri/manabi/item/ac41a799622e3efe6b28fdaad0bffa5b0b3b1b9f.pdf); 志望校-based rooms and stamps as the market's existing answer — [StudyCast listing](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/studycast-%E3%82%B9%E3%82%BF%E3%82%AD%E3%83%A3%E3%82%B9-%E5%8B%89%E5%BC%B7%E3%82%BF%E3%82%A4%E3%83%9E%E3%83%BC%E3%81%A7%E9%9B%86%E4%B8%AD%E5%8A%9B%E3%81%8C%E7%B6%99%E7%B6%9A/id1474049638)

### Inferences (feature-level)
- **Friends' feed:** show *sessions* (subject, textbook, minutes, optional page photo), not cumulative totals; default audience = mutuals + same-志望校 anonymous room; one-tap スタンプ/いいね; pause/archive state for exam season aftermath.
- **Live presence:** "N people with your goal are studying now" plus running timers; camera optional, desk-cam/avatar modes; per-session toggle to go invisible for unfamiliar work.
- **Group rooms:** persistent small cohorts keyed on 志望校・学年・教材 and time slot, with scheduled start/break rituals (ポモドーロ-like), silent by default, chat only in breaks.
