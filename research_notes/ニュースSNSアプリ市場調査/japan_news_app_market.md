# Japanese News-App Market and News Consumption Behavior (2024–2026)

Research date: 2026-11-02. Sources: Reuters Institute Digital News Report (DNR) 2024, 2025 and 2026 (Japan pages, plus the full PDFs; the numbers were pulled from the chart text, see the caveats below); the 総務省 令和6年度 media-usage survey; 新聞通信調査会 第18回 survey (2025); company IR and press coverage; and other Japanese surveys. Older data is labeled with its year.

**Note on the DNR chart numbers:** the brand reach and social-platform figures below were taken from the text layer of the official DNR PDFs. Brand labels and values are stored in reverse order there, and I matched them back by rank. Matching the "sources of news" values was confirmed against the DNR 2025 narrative text ("TV news reach in … Japan … down 3pp to 50%"). Confidence is high for the top brands and platforms, and medium for the long-tail brands.

## 1. Market size, user numbers and trends of the major Japanese news apps and aggregators

### Takeaway
Japanese online news is a two-aggregator market. Yahoo!ニュース reaches 57% of online adults weekly (DNR 2026) and LINE NEWS reaches 18%. Every publisher brand is at 13% or below. Yahoo!ニュース is still growing: about 16.5B monthly PV in FY2025 and the world's most-visited news site (May 2025). The second tier is in decline or restructuring. SmartNews cut about 40% of staff (2023) and plans a down-round IPO at about ¥50B, roughly a quarter of its 2021 valuation. Gunosy's news-curation DAU/revenue is shrinking, its KDDI portal partnership ends in March 2027, and it is pivoting グノシー toward interactive, participatory news.

### Inferences
- The free, ad-funded aggregator layer (Yahoo!, LINE) is entrenched, with 57% and 18% weekly reach. A new entrant will not win on "one place for all headlines" alone, because Yahoo!ニュース already plays that role for most adults.
- Standalone news apps (SmartNews, Gunosy) are losing ground to super-apps (Yahoo, LINE), video platforms (YouTube at 41% of online time) and AI. Their down-round and restructuring signals mean investors see weak economics in the pure aggregation model.
- Gunosy's own deck independently argues for participation, prediction, comparison and community around news. That supports the community thesis, but it comes from a declining player, not a proven winner.
- News-app engagement is shallow (4.1 min/day) and skews older men. Young users are underserved by the existing app category.

### Gaps
- No official 2025–26 MAU or DAU for Yahoo!ニュース (app), LINE NEWS, SmartNews (Japan), グノシー or ニュースパス. Companies stopped disclosing them, and Nielsen does not publish a news-app ranking publicly. data.ai and Sensor Tower Japan news-app download figures were not accessible.
- SmartNews FY2025 revenue and its pre-IPO filing were not available (the IPO is planned for December 2026; the 有価証券届出書 was not yet public).
- No 2025–26 NewsPicks paid subscriber figure was found.

## 2. How Japanese people get news, by age group

### Takeaway
At the population level, TV is still the largest single news source (51% weekly in DNR 2026) and online is 62%. For people in their 20s and 30s the internet is the first and main source: about 80% use it as their fastest news source, and they are the only age groups where it is also the top "trusted information" source. Social and video platforms for news are rising quickly in Japan (YouTube 30% weekly for news, +7pp; LINE 24%; X 20%), but they remain low by international standards. Instagram (9%) and TikTok (7%) are small for news, even though 59–78% of people in their teens and 20s use them.

### Inferences
- For 20s and 30s users the default news path is smartphone, then portal or aggregator, then SNS and video. TV and newspapers matter little to them for speed, though TV and NHK still anchor trust.
- Japan's social-news shift is behind the global curve but accelerating: YouTube +7pp, LINE +5pp and X +3pp for news in one year. Instagram and TikTok are mass-reach channels among the young (59–78%) but are barely used for news (7–9% across all adults). There is a gap between where young people spend time (visual feeds) and where they get news (Yahoo, X, YouTube). An "Instagram-style" news product would target exactly that gap.
- The rise of AI chatbots for news (9%) adds a new competitor for "summarize today's news".

### Gaps
- DNR country pages give no Japan-specific under-35 breakdowns of platform news use. They are in the DNR interactive data, which I could not access.
- The 総務省 full report (section 5-2: news-service use by age, e.g. portal vs. SNS vs. curation apps) was not extracted. Only the summary PDF was parsed. Full report: https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_content/001017160.pdf
- I found no 2025–26 Japan-specific survey that measures the share of under-30s who get news from TikTok or Instagram. The often-quoted "Gen Z 3割がTikTokでニュース" figure is from US Pew data (2023), not Japan.

## 3. News avoidance, trust in news and willingness to pay (Japan)

### Takeaway
Japan has some of the lowest news avoidance in the world (11–12% in DNR, against about 40% globally), mid-level trust (39% in 2025 and 41% in 2026, against a 37% global average), and very low willingness to pay (9–10%). About 93% pay nothing for online news, and free aggregators set the price expectation. Trust is lowest among the young: the 2025 trust drop was "especially pronounced among younger people".

### Cited Findings
- Trust "most news most of the time": 43% (2024, rank =15/47); **39% (2025, −4pp, rank 23/48, largest drop in Asia-Pacific)**; **41% (2026, +2)**, global average 37% — [DNR 2024](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2024/japan); [DNR 2025](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2025/japan); [DNR 2026](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/japan)
- DNR 2025 trust page: the drop in trust is especially pronounced "among the younger group – the very generation the media struggle to reach". NHK文研's summary says it was especially marked among 18–24 and 45–54 year olds — [DNR 2025 PDF p.145](https://www.odg.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/REUTERS-Digital_News-Report_2025_compressed.pdf); [NHK文研「放送研究と調査」2025年10月](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/bunken/75/10/75_2_27/_article/-char/ja)
- Brand trust in 2026 (trust/don't trust): NHK 57%/16%, Nikkei 51%/14%, local paper 51%, Nippon TV 50%, **Yahoo! News 46%/14%**, Yomiuri 46%, Fuji TV 46% (it had been 36% in 2025 after the scandal, −13pp), Asahi 45%, Weekly Bunshun 25%/36% — [DNR 2026 PDF](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/DNR%202026%20FINAL_2.pdf); [DNR 2025 PDF](https://www.odg.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/REUTERS-Digital_News-Report_2025_compressed.pdf)
- **Avoidance** (sometimes or often): Japan 11% in 2025, the lowest with Taiwan (21%) among the low group; global highs are Bulgaria at 63%. Japan was **12% (+1) in 2026** — [DNR 2025 PDF](https://www.odg.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/REUTERS-Digital_News-Report_2025_compressed.pdf); [DNR 2026](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/japan)
- Conflicting domestic measure: SmartNews SMPP 2025 (mail survey, n=2,117, ages 18–79) finds 18% avoid "frequently or sometimes" and 49% including "occasionally". **30s are highest at 22%**, 70s+ 20%, 40s 19%, 60s lowest at 13%. The 18–29 group had the highest share saying they "never avoid" news. Reasons (n=1,037): makes me feel depressed 61%, not interested 30%, sensational headlines 27%, too many crime stories 25%, low-effort articles that rehash TV or SNS 21% — [SmartNews SMPP 2025](https://about.smartnews.com/ja/news/2391.html). The 2023 SMPP round measured 8% avoidance, and about 60% of avoiders "don't enjoy the news" — [SMRI 2023](https://smartnews-smri.com/smppsurvey/media_value_research-2318/). The measures differ in wording and mode, so they cannot be compared directly with DNR.
- "News lovers" segment: Japan unchanged at 30% (global 22%, down from 29% in 2021) — [DNR 2026 PDF](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/DNR%202026%20FINAL_2.pdf)
- **Paying for online news:** 9% (2024), 10% (2025), **9% (2026)**. Main paid brands: Nikkei, Asahi Digital, Business Nikkei — [DNR 2024](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2024/japan); [DNR 2025](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2025/japan); [DNR 2026](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/japan)
- Among Japanese who pay, **34% have a paying relationship with an aggregator**, which is high globally — [DNR 2026 PDF](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/DNR%202026%20FINAL_2.pdf)
- 新聞通信調査会 2025: 93% use no paid internet news service, and only 4% pay for e-newspapers or articles. Trust score for internet media is 47.4 (58.0 in 2008), against NHK 66.8 and newspapers 66.2 — [新聞通信調査会 2025](https://www.chosakai.gr.jp/wp/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/018b20020e2419d1d9522b90f008a439.pdf)
- 総務省 FY2024 trust in the internet as a medium: 28.0% in the 20s and 29.1% in the 30s, against 46.8% and 43.5% for TV — [総務省 概要](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_content/001017240.pdf)
- SMRI 2023: more than 40% of 18–39 year olds said they do not trust mass media — [SMRI 2023](https://smartnews-smri.com/smppsurvey/report01-1/)

### Inferences
- Low avoidance plus high habitual aggregator use means Japanese users do check news. The problem is less "they don't want news" and more that news is consumed as a free, low-engagement habit.
- Willingness to pay below 10% means a consumer subscription news app faces a hard ceiling. The model would need to be ad- or community-funded or freemium. Aggregator payments (34% of payers) suggest that bundles or premium features on a platform can monetize better than single-publisher paywalls.
- Avoidance peaks among people in their 30s (SMPP 22%), mainly for emotional reasons and sensationalism. A calmer, curated, finishable format could address this.

### Gaps
- Japan-specific DNR age-group splits for trust, avoidance and payment were not available from the country page or PDF.
- No reliable data on Japanese willingness to pay for community or social features specifically.

## 4. Gen Z: summarized, short-form and visual news, and demand for "finishable" daily digests

### Takeaway
Evidence for Japanese Gen Z news habits is mostly indirect. The young are heavy users of TikTok and Instagram (59–78%) and AI, and strongly time-efficiency (タイパ) oriented. Broadcasters now run large TikTok news accounts (Nippon TV News about 2.9M followers). But Japan-specific quantitative evidence on demand for summarized news or "daily digest / newsletter" formats is thin. DNR shows the Japanese public ranks "adapting reading levels" low among desired AI features, while "making news easier to understand" ranks relatively high.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok use by age: 10代 65.7%, 20代 58.7% (総務省 FY2024). Instagram: 10代 75.0%, 20代 78.0% — [総務省 概要](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_content/001017240.pdf). New adults' TikTok use is 61.2% (December 2024) — [マクロミル](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000666.000000624.html)
- Yet weekly news use is only 7% on TikTok and 9% on Instagram across all adults (2026) — [DNR 2026 PDF](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/DNR%202026%20FINAL_2.pdf). Globally, DNR finds most people on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok "only see news when they are online for other reasons"; only X and YouTube are seen as useful for news by more than half their users — [DNR 2026 PDF](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/DNR%202026%20FINAL_2.pdf)
- Short-form news by Japanese broadcasters: the 日テレNEWS TikTok account has about 2.9M followers and 152.4M likes (figure at time of access) — [TikTok @ntv.news](https://www.tiktok.com/@ntv.news?lang=ja-JP). An industry blog reports テレビ朝日 news at about 2.38M TikTok followers, with the fastest growth (date unclear) — [gaaaon blog](https://gaaaon.jp/blog/tiktok_news)
- During the 2025 Upper House and 2026 general elections, "the surge of short-form video… presented new challenges for broadcasters". NHK published unedited speech footage and full transcripts — [DNR 2026 Japan](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/japan)
- DNR 2025 (AI and personalisation chapter): Japan is one of the markets where "adapting the language in news articles for different reading levels ranks in the bottom three" of desired AI features — [DNR 2025 PDF](https://www.odg.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/REUTERS-Digital_News-Report_2025_compressed.pdf). DNR 2026: "making news easier to understand" is a top use of AI in Austria and "also ranks highly in Germany and Japan" — [DNR 2026 PDF](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/DNR%202026%20FINAL_2.pdf)
- AI chatbots for news in Japan doubled to 9% weekly (2026) — [DNR 2026](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2026-06/DNR%202026%20FINAL_2.pdf). ChatGPT for smartphone search among new adults: 30.8% (+19.4pt) — [マクロミル](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000666.000000624.html)
- Time-efficiency mindset (teens, セイコー時間白書2025): 49.0% "don't want to waste even one minute" and 61.5% "work harder with time limits". But 74.3% "want to think for myself even if the answer isn't immediate" — [セイコー時間白書2025](https://www.seiko.co.jp/csr/stda/archive/2025/detail.html)
- Gunosy's FY26 strategy names short video, AI-optimized feeds and SNS as the new mode of information acquisition, and frames news as needing to become "エンタメ化された情報取得体験" — [Gunosy FY26 deck](https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/60470/140120260715594110.pdf)
- US contrast (not Japan): 32% of US 18–29s regularly got news from TikTok in 2023 (9% in 2020) — [Pew via Business Insider Japan](https://www.businessinsider.jp/article/280202/)

### Inferences
- The visual-feed habit is strong among Japanese Gen Z, but news on those feeds is mostly incidental. A product that delivers news inside an Instagram-like visual format meets the young where they already are. Whether they will open a dedicated news app daily is unproven, and app-category data (4.1 min/day, older skew) is a caution.
- AI summaries (chatbots, Yahoo!/SmartNews AI features) are commoditizing "today's news summarized". Differentiation would need to come from curation voice, a finishable format and social context, not from summarization alone.

### Gaps
- **I found no Japan-specific 2024–26 survey quantifying demand for "summarized news", "1日1回 digests" or news newsletters among Gen Z.** I also found no Japan data on Axios-style or newsletter adoption.
- No verified 2025–26 follower or view figures for 日経 or ABEMA short-news accounts. ABEMA appears in DNR only as a 3–4% weekly online brand.
- No Japanese data separating "news from creators" by age (DNR 2026 says creator impact in Japan is low overall).

## 5. Signals of unmet need: dissatisfaction, overload, comment toxicity, desire to discuss news

### Takeaway
There are clear dissatisfaction signals. News apps are rated poorly (only 1.4% rated 4.5 or higher), avoiders cite negativity and sensationalism, trust in internet media is low among 20s and 30s (about 28–29%), and Yahoo!ニュース comments have a long-running toxicity problem that needs AI moderation at scale. Japan's most-used comment space (ヤフコメ, about 111M comments a year) shows that discussion demand exists. Its reputation also shows the risk of a toxic comment layer. Direct survey evidence of a desire to "discuss news with others / in groups" was not found.

### Cited Findings
- Yahoo!ニュース comments: **111.272M comments posted in FY2023**, with 1.378M deleted (about 1.2%) — [LINEヤフー 2023 media transparency report, via Web担当者Forum](https://webtan.impress.co.jp/n/2024/10/03/47847). About 10M posts per month, of which about 2.4% were removed as inappropriate (2023 report) — [Web担当者Forum 2023-10](https://webtan.impress.co.jp/n/2023/10/05/45740). Volume history: about 140k per day (2015) → about 280k per day (2018) → 330k+ per day (2020) — [Yahoo!ニュース コメントプロジェクト](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/newshack/inside/yjnews_comment2018.html); [Yahoo PR 2020](https://about.yahoo.co.jp/pr/release/2020/09/18a/)
- The AI "コメント添削モデル" (launched September 2024) asks users to reconsider offensive comments. Offensive comments fell about 24%, and about 50% of flagged comments were rewritten in a less offensive form — [LINEヤフー release (2025-02)](https://www.lycorp.co.jp/ja/news/release/016951/); [DNR 2025 Japan](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2025/japan)
- August 2025: policy-violation deletions fell to less than a quarter of their earlier level after the cleanup measures (comparison period not confirmed) — [日テレNEWS via Yahoo!ニュース](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/d62053f5688003626c5cca64c7def5e67fb76a92)
- Since October 2021, Yahoo! automatically hides the comment section on articles with many violating comments: 216 articles in the first two months (about 3.5 per day) — [Web担当者Forum 2022](https://webtan.impress.co.jp/n/2022/01/21/42237)
- News apps are rated poorly: 1.4% of news and newspaper apps are rated 4.5–5.0, against 14.4% across all apps (App Ape, June 2025) — [フラー](https://note.fuller-inc.com/n/n6efb4080e1fa)
- Gunosy's own diagnosis: news experiences are "面白みがなく継続しづらい", one-way, hard to make personal, and get buried in volume (commoditization) — [Gunosy FY26 deck](https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/60470/140120260715594110.pdf)
- Avoidance reasons (SMPP 2025): depressing 61%, sensational headlines 27%, too much crime 25%, low-quality rehashed content 21%. 18% avoid emotional or outrage-inducing news — [SmartNews SMPP 2025](https://about.smartnews.com/ja/news/2391.html)
- SNS seen as answering "what I want to know" better than legacy media by people in their 40s and under, but as carrying the most misinformation. 28% think SNS reveals "the truth" better than mass media (higher among the young) — [新聞通信調査会 2025](https://www.chosakai.gr.jp/wp/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/018b20020e2419d1d9522b90f008a439.pdf)
- SMRI 2023: about 80% of internet users have SNS accounts, but half of all respondents judge news received through SNS as "not important" — [SMRI 2023](https://smartnews-smri.com/smppsurvey/report01-2-sns/)
- Low social sharing: only 13% share news through social media, messaging or email weekly (2025) — [DNR 2025 Japan](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2025/japan)
- Polarization signal: the Hyogo 2024 and 2026 elections showed online communities organizing around distrust of legacy media. Reporters covering Saito's rally were confronted and filmed by his supporters — [DNR 2025 Japan](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2025/japan); [DNR 2026 Japan](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/japan)

### Inferences
- About 111M comments a year on Yahoo!ニュース alone suggests large latent demand to react to and discuss news. That demand is currently met in an open, anonymous, often toxic venue. Closed or semi-closed groups (friends, interest communities) and identity-anchored discussion are a plausible gap, but this is inference, not measured demand.
- Japanese users share news rarely (13%) and favor closed messaging (LINE is the #2 social news platform at 24%). Group- and DM-based discussion may fit Japanese norms better than public posting.
- Moderation is a core cost and a reputational risk. LINEヤフー needed AI deletion, AI rewrite prompts and 24/7 patrol to manage comments.

### Gaps
- No Japanese survey found that directly measures desire to "discuss news with friends/communities" or satisfaction with ヤフコメ among young users.
- No 2025 full-year Yahoo! comment volume found (latest is FY2023's 111M).
- No data found on news-related community features in Japanese apps (e.g. NewsPicks' comment model engagement in 2025–26, or Threads news discussion in Japan).
