# Global Case Studies: News + Social / Visual-Short-Form News Apps (2023–2026), with lessons for a Japanese "Instagram for news"

Research date: 2026-11-02. All dates in brackets are the date of the fact/report, not of retrieval. Figures marked "(secondary)" come from aggregators or third-party trackers and were not verified against primary filings.

---

## Q1. Failures and why (Artifact, Facebook News tab, Google+/Currents, Digg relaunch, Yahoo comments, Clubhouse)

### Takeaway
The recurring failure modes are: (a) a standalone general-news app cannot reach a big enough audience to justify venture-scale investment (Artifact), (b) platforms deprioritize news when it is a small, politically/legally costly share of engagement (Facebook News), (c) open voting/commenting systems are overrun by bots, spam and toxicity faster than small teams can moderate (Digg 2026, Yahoo comments), and (d) hype-driven social formats collapse once the novelty context disappears (Clubhouse).

### Cited Findings

**Artifact (Kevin Systrom & Mike Krieger, launched early 2023, shutdown announced Jan 2024)**
- Systrom stated the company was winding down because "the market opportunity isn't big enough to warrant continued investment in this way" [Jan 12, 2024]. Posting/commenting stopped immediately; reading continued until end of Feb 2024 — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-founders-are-shutting-down-artifact-their-year-old-news-app-233431390.html); [Wikipedia: Artifact (app)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artifact_(app))
- Note: Artifact had added social features (user posts, comments, links sharing) in 2023 before shutting down; posts/comments were the first things turned off — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-founders-are-shutting-down-artifact-their-year-old-news-app-233431390.html)
- Yahoo acquired Artifact's technology (not the brand/app as a standalone) in April 2024; Systrom and Krieger advised during transition; Artifact's AI personalization was folded into a revamped Yahoo News app launched in 2024 — [Wikipedia: Artifact (app)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artifact_(app)); [btw.media](https://btw.media/en/yahoo-purchases-ai-news-platform-artefact-from-instagram-co-founders)
- The app actually lingered past the stated shutdown date; reports in late March 2024 said it was still delivering news daily — [Thurrott](https://www.thurrott.com/?p=300058)

**Facebook News tab (Meta)**
- Meta deprecated the dedicated Facebook News tab in the UK, France and Germany in late 2023 (Dec 2023), and announced (Feb 29/Mar 1, 2024) deprecation in the US and Australia in early April 2024 — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/meta-is-killing-the-facebook-news-tab-in-the-us-and-australia-082750820.html); [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2024/03/01/meta-is-deprecating-facebook-news-in-the-us-and-australia-as-it-has-done-in-europe)
- Stated reasons: usage of Facebook News in the US and Australia dropped by over 80% in the prior year; separately, news makes up less than 3% of what people see in their Facebook feed globally (these are two different statistics). Meta said it would focus on what people want more of, "including short-form video" — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2024/03/01/facebook-to-close-its-news-tabs-in-the-us-and-australia-and-will-focus-on-short-form-video); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/meta-is-killing-the-facebook-news-tab-in-the-us-and-australia-082750820.html)
- Meta said it would not enter new commercial deals for traditional news content in those countries; news links remain viewable — [Storyboard18](https://stgcms.storyboard18.com/?p=24922)

**Google+ / Google Currents**
- Google announced (Feb 2022) it would wind down Google Currents (the enterprise successor to Google+) in 2023 and migrate communities to Google Chat "Spaces"; shutdown for Workspace was July 5, 2023. Stated reason: consolidation into Spaces — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/companies/google-to-shut-down-currents-in-2023-users-to-be-moved-to-spaces-122021100917_1.html); [Wikipedia: Google Currents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Currents_(social_app))
- (Separate product, for context) Google's earlier "Google Currents" news reader was folded into Google Play Newsstand → Google News — not researched further here.

**Digg relaunch by Kevin Rose & Alexis Ohanian (2025–2026) — current status**
- Mar 5, 2025: Rose and Ohanian acquired what remained of the original Digg via a leveraged buyout involving True Ventures, Seven Seven Six (Ohanian), S32, and the founders personally; amount undisclosed. Intention: a site where communities have more moderator/admin control and ownership — [TechCrunch, Mar 13 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/13/digg-lays-off-staff-and-shuts-down-app-as-company-retools)
- Open beta launched publicly Jan 14, 2026; shut down ~2 months later (Mar 13–14, 2026) — [Wikipedia: Digg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digg); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/13/digg-lays-off-staff-and-shuts-down-app-as-company-retools)
- Reasons given by CEO Justin Mezzell: (1) an "unprecedented bot problem" — SEO spammers targeted Digg because it still carried Google link authority; bots undermined vote-based ranking; Digg banned "tens of thousands of accounts," built tooling and used outside vendors but "we didn't appreciate the scale, sophistication, or speed at which they'd find us"; "The internet is now populated, in meaningful part, by sophisticated AI agents and automated accounts... This isn't just a Digg problem. It's an internet problem." (2) "the brutal reality of finding product-market fit in an environment that has fundamentally changed"; competing with incumbents (likely Reddit) was "not just a moat but a wall" — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/13/digg-lays-off-staff-and-shuts-down-app-as-company-retools); [Wikipedia: Digg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digg)
- Status as of Mar 2026: large share of staff laid off (headcount undisclosed); app pulled from App Store; website showed only the layoff post; company says it is NOT closing — Kevin Rose returns full-time with a small team to rebuild something "genuinely different"; Diggnation podcast continues — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/13/digg-lays-off-staff-and-shuts-down-app-as-company-retools)
- Some outlets characterized it as a shutdown ("Digg Shuts Down After 2 Months as AI Bot Spam Overwhelms Platform") — [TechBuzz](https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/digg-shuts-down-after-2-months-as-ai-bot-spam-overwhelms-platform)

**Yahoo News comment section**
- Yahoo suspended comments on news articles in 2020 (sources disagree: July 23, 2020 for the international site vs. October 2020); reasons cited were user experience/safety, spam, and moderation cost of heavy comment volume — [Republic World](https://www.republicworld.com/tech/apps/what-happened-to-yahoo-comments-section-why-did-yahoo-remove-comments); [Distractify](https://www.distractify.com/p/yahoo-comments-gone) (both secondary; no primary Yahoo statement retrieved)

**Clubhouse**
- Downloads peaked in Q1 2021 (reported ~14 million downloads that quarter) then plateaued; in April 2023 Clubhouse laid off nearly half its staff to "reset" the product — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/clubhouses-billion-dollar-balloon-deflates-201848479.html)

### Inferences
- Artifact is the most directly relevant cautionary tale for an "Instagram for news": a world-class team with Instagram pedigree, AI personalization AND social features (posts, comments) still concluded a general news-discovery app was not a venture-scale market. The social layer did not create enough retention on its own.
- Facebook's retreat shows news is a low-engagement, high-liability category even inside the largest social network; a news-first social app must create reasons to return that are not just "news" (identity, community, local utility, creators).
- Digg 2026 shows that in the AI-agent era, open sign-up + vote/comment ranking is attacked immediately; anti-bot/identity verification must be a launch-day feature, not a later fix. Japan-specific: Yahoo! News Japan comments (out of scope here) are a known analog for toxicity concerns.
- Clubhouse shows that a format-driven (rather than need-driven) social product decays once novelty fades; "events" features should be anchored to recurring news moments (elections, earnings, sports) rather than generic live rooms.

### Gaps
- No Artifact user numbers found (never disclosed publicly in sources retrieved).
- No news after March 2026 on Digg's rebuild/relaunch timeline was found; status as of Nov 2026 unverified.
- No primary Yahoo statement on comment removal retrieved; current status of Yahoo News (US) comments not verified.

## Q2. Successes / active players (with users, revenue, funding)

### Takeaway
Players that sustain are either (a) massive platforms where news is one topic among many (Reddit, Instagram/TikTok/YouTube), (b) niche/utility products with a clear monetization model (Ground News' bias-comparison subscription, Morning Brew/Skimm finishable newsletters monetized via ads/B2B), or (c) local/hyperlocal utility (Nextdoor's 2025 "News + Alerts" relaunch, India's Public app). Pure AI-summary readers (Particle) are still small and venture-funded.

### Cited Findings

- Stock down >80% since 2021 SPAC listing (context for relaunch) — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91367794/inside-the-redesign-that-will-make-you-actually-want-to-use-nextdoor)
- Metrics: old WAU 46.1M (Q4 2024); new stricter "Platform WAU" 21.0M (Q4 2025); FY2025 revenue $258M; Q2 2026 Platform WAU reported at 22.9M (+5% YoY; secondary, unverified) — [Expanded Ramblings (secondary)](https://expandedramblings.com/index.php/nextdoor-statistics/)

**Daily digests ("finishable" news)**
- Morning Brew: flagship newsletter >4M subscribers (Oct 2024); revenue ~$70M by end of 2024 with ~20% EBITDA margin (per founder Austin Rief, secondary); fully owned by Axel Springer since Feb 2025; B2B division expected to surpass flagship revenue — [Axios, Oct 2024](https://www.axios.com/2024/10/22/morning-brew-newsletter-b2b-brands); [News Machines (secondary)](https://newsmachines.substack.com/p/how-morning-brew-uses-data-to-grow)
- theSkimm: ~7M subscribers across platforms at 2024 peak; acquired by Ziff Davis in March 2025 — [The Zero to One (secondary)](https://www.thezerotoone.co/p/how-morning-brew-grew-subscribers); [readless (secondary)](https://www.readless.app/newsletters/the-skimm)
- Axios: sold to Cox Enterprises in 2022 for $525M — [The Zero to One (secondary)](https://www.thezerotoone.co/p/how-morning-brew-grew-subscribers)

### Inferences
- Revenue models that work: subscription for a distinctive utility (Ground News bias view), ads + B2B verticals on finishable digests (Morning Brew), local ads (Nextdoor), ad-driven hyperlocal video at mass scale (Public). Social features alone are not a monetization model.
- Morning Brew and theSkimm both ended up acquired by larger media groups (Axel Springer, Ziff Davis) — daily-digest brands scale to tens of millions in revenue but plateau as standalone companies.
- Nextdoor's 2025 pivot is the strongest evidence that "news" works as a retention hook when attached to an existing community graph (neighbors) and utility (alerts), not vice versa.
- Reddit's continued growth (news subreddits sit inside a broad interest-community platform) suggests news discussion thrives as one vertical within identity/interest communities rather than as the organizing principle.

## Q3. Gen Z / young people's news habits globally (Reuters DNR 2025/2026, Pew)

### Takeaway
Social/video platforms and creators are now the dominant news gateway for under-25s globally; Instagram is the #1 social platform for news among 18–24s (DNR 2026); AI chatbots are a fast-growing but still minor source. Japan is an outlier where news sites/apps (aggregators) still lead and creator news use is low.

### Cited Findings

**Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2026 (published June 2026, 48 markets)** — [DNR 2026 executive summary](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/dnr-executive-summary)
- 54% use social/video networks for news weekly — first time ahead of news websites/apps (51%); 30% name social/video as main source (22% five years ago); 12% use only social/video for news (6% in 2020); social beats news sites/apps in 30 of 48 markets.
- Platform news use: Facebook 43%, YouTube 34%, Instagram 26%, TikTok 20%; TikTok news >50% in Thailand and Kenya; X news use fell 7pp in the US.
- 77% watch online news video weekly; online news video beats broadcast TV news in 45 markets; video on publishers' own sites/apps fell 5pp to 23%.
- News creators: 27% get news weekly from news-focused creators, 46% from creators of any type; 13% say most/all needs met by news creators; creator use highest in Asia, Africa, Latin America, lowest in Europe and Japan.
- 18–24s: 52% say social media, video networks or AI chatbots are their main source; Instagram is the largest social platform for news among 18–24s (42%); X among 18–24s fell to 15%.
- AI chatbots: 10% weekly for news (7% in 2025); 16% among under-35s; 42% of chatbot news users always/often click through to sources; most popular feature is follow-up questions (42%); trust in chatbot answers 20% globally.
- Trust in news 37% (lowest since 2015); trust in news on social media 22%; 42% sometimes/often avoid news; 17% pay for online news (20-country basket).
- Japan: news websites/apps still lead social/video; 30% are "news lovers"; 34% of Japanese news payers pay for aggregators (relatively high); creator news use lower than global average.
- Also: [GIJN summary of DNR 2026](https://gijn.org/stories/2026-reuters-institute-digital-news-report/)

**Reuters Institute DNR 2025 (June 2025)**
- Social video news consumption grew from 52% (2020) to 65% (2025); in the US, social/video networks (54%) overtook TV (50%) and news sites/apps (48%) for the first time; 44% of 18–24s say social media is their main news source; TikTok weekly news use 17% (+4pp); YouTube 30%; 22% of US sample encountered Joe Rogan discussing news in the prior week; influencers and politicians each seen as top false-information threats (47%) — [DNR 2025 executive summary](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2025/dnr-executive-summary); [Press Gazette](https://pressgazette.co.uk/publishers/news-trends-2025-digital-news-report/)
- Reuters Institute also published a 2025 study mapping news creators/influencers across countries — [Reuters Institute](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/news-creators-influencers/2025/mapping-news-creators-and-influencers-social-and-video-networks)

**Pew Research Center (US)**
- Sep 25, 2025: 20% of US adults regularly get news on TikTok (3% in 2020); 43% of adults under 30 (9% in 2020); 55% of TikTok users regularly get news there (22% in 2020); survey Aug 18–24, 2025, n=5,153 — [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/09/25/1-in-5-americans-now-regularly-get-news-on-tiktok-up-sharply-from-2020/); [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2025/09/more-americans-than-ever-now-get-news-on-tiktok-pew-finds/)
- Dec 3, 2025 "Young Adults and the Future of News": 38% of adults under 30 regularly get news from news influencers — [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/journalism/2025/12/03/young-adults-and-the-future-of-news/)
- Jan 2025: closer look at TikTok news experiences — TikTok users follow far more entertainment/pop culture accounts than news accounts — [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/01/17/a-closer-look-at-americans-experiences-with-news-on-tiktok/)

### Inferences
- An Instagram-like visual format is aligned with where 18–24s already get news (Instagram 42% among 18–24s globally per DNR 2026). But competing head-on with Instagram/TikTok for attention is the challenge; the differentiator must be trust, finishability, and discussion quality.
- Japan is structurally different: aggregator-led (Yahoo! News, SmartNews, LINE NEWS) with low creator news use — a Japanese "Instagram for news" may need to bridge aggregator habits (curated digest) with social, rather than assume a creator-led market like the US/Asia-Pacific/LatAm.
- Low trust in news on social media (22%) is an opening for a product that visibly labels sources and corrections.

### Gaps
- DNR 2026 figures on commenting/participation in news not extracted (not in the retrieved executive summary portion).
- No Japan-specific Gen Z figures collected here (out of scope; other researchers likely cover).

## Q4. AI summarization news apps — opportunities and risks

### Takeaway
AI summaries are a real user draw (Particle, Artifact tech in Yahoo News, chatbots as news source growing to 10% weekly), but hallucinated summaries of news carry outsized reputational and legal risk, as Apple's January 2025 suspension showed.

### Cited Findings
- Apple Intelligence notification summaries produced false news alerts: a BBC-attributed summary claimed Luigi Mangione had shot himself; another said Luke Littler had won the PDC World Darts Championship before the final; another claimed Rafael Nadal had come out as gay. BBC filed a complaint Dec 2024; RSF urged removal — [MacRumors, Jan 16 2025](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/01/16/ios-18-3-news-notifications-removed/); [Mediaite](https://www.mediaite.com/media/news/apple-suspends-ai-news-alerts-after-false-bbc-story-claimed-luigi-mangione-shot-himself); [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/apple-pauses-ai-generated-news-summaries-after-serious-accuracy-issues-surface/)
- Apple paused summaries for News & Entertainment apps in iOS 18.3 (Jan 2025), added a "beta, may contain errors" warning, and italicized summaries to distinguish them from publisher text — [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/01/16/ios-18-3-news-notifications-removed/)
- Artifact's AI features (personalization; it had also tested AI summaries/rewriting clickbait headlines) were acquired by Yahoo and folded into the Yahoo News app (2024) — [Wikipedia: Artifact (app)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artifact_(app))
- Particle's model: multi-source AI summaries per story with publisher partnerships and freemium Particle+ — [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/reading/ai-news-reader-particle-adds-publishing-partners-and-10-9m-in-new-funding/); [Particle blog](https://particle.news/blog/particle-news-launches-android-app-bringing-fact-forward-personalized-news-to-more-users-worldwide)
- DNR 2026: chatbot news use 10% weekly (17% of 18–24s per secondary summary), only 1% name AI their main source; trust in chatbot answers 20% (6% UK); 42% of chatbot news users click through to sources; follow-up questions most valued feature — [DNR 2026](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/dnr-executive-summary); [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/more-people-get-news-from-ai-chatbots-but-trust-remains-low/)
- Digg 2026: AI agents/bots also pose a supply-side risk to social news (fake engagement, spam) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/13/digg-lays-off-staff-and-shuts-down-app-as-company-retools)

### Inferences
- For a Japanese digest app: AI summaries should be clearly labeled, link to the original article, never be pushed as notifications without human review for breaking/sensitive news (deaths, crimes, elections), and ideally be generated under publisher agreements (Particle model) to avoid copyright disputes.
- "Ask follow-up questions" on an article is the most-valued chatbot behavior (DNR 2026) — a natural feature to embed next to comment sections.
- AI-generated bot accounts are the biggest threat to comment/community features (Digg); identity verification (e.g., phone/My Number-style or invite) and rate-limits should be core.

### Gaps
- No data found on Yahoo News app usage/accuracy after integrating Artifact tech.
- Whether Apple restored news summaries in later iOS versions (iOS 18.4+/iOS 26) was not verified.

## Q5. Patterns that correlate with sustaining community around news

### Takeaway
Community around news is sustained when news is attached to a pre-existing identity or place (neighborhood, interest, profession, ideology-comparison), when there is a creator/personality layer, when there is a utility reason to return daily (alerts, finishable digest), and when moderation and anti-bot measures are strong from day one. General-purpose "news + comments" without those anchors repeatedly fails.

### Cited Findings
- Local/utility anchor: Nextdoor rebuilt around News + real-time Alerts + local recommendations in 2025 to restore daily use — [Nextdoor](https://about.nextdoor.com/press-releases/meet-the-new-nextdoor); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/07/15/nextdoor-app-ai-reboot)
- Hyperlocal UGC video: India's Public app (user/stringer-shot local video) reached tens of millions of users with 20–30 min/day engagement — [The Ken](https://the-ken.com/story/inshorts-bumpy-public-road-to-crowdsourced-video-news-greatness/)
- Interest-community scale: Reddit at 130.3M DAUq (Q2 2026), with news subreddits embedded in broad interest communities — [Reddit Q2 2026](https://s203.q4cdn.com/380862485/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/Q2-26-Earnings-Press-Release.pdf)

### Inferences (concrete lessons for a Japanese "Instagram for news")
1. **Don't be a general news app with comments bolted on.** Artifact + Facebook News prove the market for generic news discovery is too small/low-engagement. Anchor communities to identity: local area (prefecture/city/station), interest verticals (economy/careers, entertainment, sports, tech), schools/workplaces, or life stage.
2. **Finishable daily digest as the habit loop** (Morning Brew/Skimm/Axios model): a bounded "you're done for today" digest is a proven retention format; social features hang off each item.
3. **Visual/short-form is aligned with Gen Z (Instagram #1 for news among 18–24s, DNR 2026), but Japan is aggregator-led and low in creator news use** — expect slower creator-led growth; consider partnering with existing news creators and publishers rather than relying on UGC news.
4. **Utility hooks for daily return**: local alerts (disaster/weather/train delays — highly relevant in Japan), event features tied to recurring news moments, not generic live audio rooms (Clubhouse decline).
5. **Anti-bot and moderation from day one**: Digg's 2026 collapse in ~2 months from AI bots; Yahoo comment suspension. Use verified identity, reputation, rate-limiting, AI-assisted moderation, and community moderators with real ownership.
6. **AI summaries: labeled, sourced, publisher-licensed, no unreviewed push alerts on sensitive news** (Apple Jan 2025).
7. **Monetization must be designed early**: subscription for a distinctive utility (Ground News-style bias/perspective comparison), local ads (Nextdoor), B2B/vertical newsletters (Morning Brew), or ad-driven mass hyperlocal video (Public). Japan has a relatively high share of payers paying for aggregators (34% of payers, DNR 2026) — a possible opening for premium tiers.
8. **Distribution via creators** (Ground News on YouTube) can be cheaper than paid UA for news apps.
